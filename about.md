# About

I'm Kritin Sathi, a software engineer in San Francisco.

I write here when I've measured something and the result surprised me — usually
about AI agent tooling, command-line debugging, or macOS internals.

Two things I try to hold to:

**Every number carries its scope.** If I write "median," it's a median, and the
denominator is stated. Most of what I publish comes out of instrumentation I
built for my own use, so the sample sizes are what they are and I say so.

**The retractions stay in.** Several posts here include a section on what I got
wrong on the way to the result — usually a mechanism I invented to explain two
observations that were both real. Those sections are the point, not an apology.

Corrections are welcome and I'll credit them.
